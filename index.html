Roof Mildew & Algae Removal

Real drone footage and photos from a North City Roofing job.

🚁 Drone footage

Those black streaks aren't dirt.

Dark streaks and mildew on a roof are usually algae. Besides making the house look older, they hold moisture against the shingles. On this home our crew treated the whole roof by hand, at low pressure — no harsh power-washing that strips shingle granules.

  • Full drone and on-roof inspection first
  • Low-pressure treatment, section by section
  • Careful work around vents, flashing and gutters
